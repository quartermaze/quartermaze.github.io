Privacy Policy - Quarter Maze

Last updated: 2026/11/02

Quarter Maze ("the game") is published by Zwenk. Contact: quartermaze@gmail.com.

1. Information the game keeps on your device

The game saves your progress, records, settings, coins, upgrades and unlocked items in a file on your device. We do not receive this file and we do not send it to any server. Uninstalling the game deletes it. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may also save this file in your own Google account backup, so your progress comes back if you change phone or reinstall the game; we cannot access that backup.

2. What the game does not do

The game has no in-app purchases, does not use analytics or tracking tools of its own, does not create accounts and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

3. Advertising (optional rewarded video)

The game offers one kind of advertising: when you run out of lives you may choose to watch a short video ad to continue your run once. No ads are shown unless you choose to watch one. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID and similar identifiers, your approximate location (from your IP address) and information about how the ad is used, under the Google Privacy Policy. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice from the game's options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, the game sends your scores to Google Play Games Services so that leaderboards work. Google processes your player ID, display name and profile image under the Google Privacy Policy. Your display name and score can be shown to other players on the leaderboards. You can sign out or delete your Play Games profile and data from the Google Play Games app and from your Google account settings. The game works without signing in.

5. Permissions

The game uses the vibration of your device (a short vibration when something happens), the motion sensor (gravity/accelerometer) only to tilt the board in the TILT mode, and network access for the optional ads and for Google Play Games. Motion readings stay on your device, are used only while you play and are never stored or sent anywhere. It does not read your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy: quartermaze@gmail.com.
